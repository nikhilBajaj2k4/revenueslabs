A little more clarity

Before we talk.

A few answers to the questions you might have.

What do you need from us?

A clear offer, an idea of your ideal customer and feedback on the campaign copy. We’ll work through the details together on the kickoff call.

Who handles prospect replies?

Your team handles replies and ongoing conversations. We take care of campaign execution and follow-up sequences.

Who is this a good fit for?

Agencies, consultants and B2B businesses with a defined offer and room to take on new clients. We’ll assess fit on the intro call.

What happens before launch?

We agree on your target audience, set up the infrastructure and prepare your copy. You review and approve the messaging before campaigns go live.
