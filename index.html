FAQ

Questions

Who is this for?
B2B service businesses, agencies, founders, and sales teams that want a reliable outbound channel without building an in-house outbound team.
What does the $500 base cover?
The base covers setup, targeting, copy, launch, inbox monitoring, and campaign management so outbound keeps running without needing your team to operate it daily.
How does the meeting fee work?
We agree on one fixed fee per qualified meeting before launch. Once the campaign is live, the performance side of the pricing is tied to meetings that match that definition.
What counts as a qualified meeting?
We define it together before launch so the target role, company fit, market, and intent level are clear before a single email goes out.
